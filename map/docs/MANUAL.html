RackReach Map · version 1.1.0 · map study

User manual

How the RackReach Map works, screen by screen. It is the map half of RackReach and nothing else: you load the design books (design book), confirm the scales, place and move racks and the other markers by hand, measure, and export the marked-up map as a PDF. There is no demand list, no matching, no demand and no coverage calculation here. The version history is in CHANGELOG.md.

What it is for

A drawing board for rack research on the venue design books: where would a rack go, how far does it reach, what does a level look like with these racks.

You import the books, the application reads the scale of each sheet, the room names and the racks the design already states, and from then on everything is visual. You place racks (critical or non-critical), equipment rooms, IDFs, COWs, AV network nodes, Wi-Fi points and named areas, move them, measure distances on the sheet's own scale and look at the reach circles around every rack. When the picture is right you export it as a marked-up PDF with the legend.

Nothing here is counted. A Wi-Fi point is a mark, not an access point count; a rack serves no space; no port is computed. The demand list is not read at all.

Two separate applications

The RackReach Map and RackReach are installed side by side and share nothing:

The workflow

  1. Install — unzip the package and double-click setup-noadmin.bat (no administrator rights needed), or run setup.bat as administrator. Details in the installation manual.
  2. Projects — create the project (name, venue code).
  3. Import — load one or more books (PDF). Several series (300, 400, 600…) can live in one project.
  4. Scale — tick the key plans, confirm the scales, and if you want the racks the drawings already state, press Place racks from the drawing.
  5. Drawing viewer — the work: draw, move, measure, look at the reach circles.
  6. Racks — the list: rename, set critical or non-critical, delete, jump to a page.
  7. Report (PDF) — the marked-up PDF, and the composite map if you built one.

Step 1

Import

ControlWhat it does
1 · Drawings (PDF)Choose one book or several at once. They are read one after the other: pages, title blocks, scales, room names, rack symbols and codes. The same file twice, or a name already in the project, is refused. Remove takes a book out with what is drawn on it.
2 · Distance circlesThe radius of the two reach circles drawn around every rack — the general limit (90 m by default) and the photographer limit (70 m) — and the units distances are shown in. Save applies them everywhere, the PDF included.
Step 2

Scale

Every measure and every circle depends on the sheet's scale, so confirm it before you trust a distance.

ControlWhat it does
Key planMarks a sheet that only illustrates (key enlarged plan, index, abbreviations). Its rooms are not read and Place racks from the drawing skips it; you can still draw on it by hand. Ticked from the title block; change it and press Apply key-plan ticks.
Set (ratio or text) / ConfirmType 200 or 1/8" = 1'-0" to override the detected scale; Confirm applies it (or the detected one) and marks the page confirmed.
Confirm all pagesConfirms every page that has a scale, in one go.
Place racks from the drawingPlaces the racks the books state — team annotations, TEC.010 / TEC.020 (or TEC.003.01 / .02) rooms, "Network Equipment Rack" labels, rack block symbols — never on a key plan and never where a rack already is. They are named in sequence (RKc_01.1 …, RK_20.1 …). Running it again only adds what is missing. Optional: you can draw every rack yourself instead.
Step 3

Drawing viewer

Where the work is done. The page selector, the key-plan switch and the search are at the top; the tools are in the column on the left; the inspector is on the right.

Tools

ToolWhat it does
selectClick a marker, a room or an area to see it in the inspector; drag an unlocked marker to move it; drag the sheet to pan; the wheel zooms.
+Rack★ / +rackPlaces a critical (red) or a non-critical (yellow) rack, with its reach circles. It is named in sequence; rename it in the inspector or on the racks screen. Giving it the name of another rack asks whether it is the same rack on another page.
+Equipment room / +IDF / +COW / +AV networkMarks what is in the building. No circles.
+Wi-FiDraws the Wi-Fi sign where you click, to illustrate a space with wireless. No name and nothing counted. Stays armed: click again for the next one.
+areaA named area (BTOC, a compound): click each corner, double-click or press Enter to close it, then name it.
measureClick each corner; double-click or Enter ends it; Backspace takes back a point; Esc drops it. Measured on this sheet's scale; nothing is saved or exported. Clear measures takes them all off the sheet.
deleteThe eraser: click a marker, a Wi-Fi point, an area or a measure to delete it. Stays on until you choose select. A selected item also goes with the Delete key.

ButtonWhat it does

Step 4

Racks

The list of racks: name, criticality, pages, delete.

ColumnWhat it does
NameRename it; the marker on the map follows. A name another rack already has asks whether they are the same rack (OK joins them) or keeps the old name.
CriticalityCritical or non-critical — the colour on the map and in the PDF.
PagesEvery page it is drawn on; click one to open it in the Drawing viewer, centred on the rack.
Delete / Removed (n) / RestoreDeletes the rack from every page; removed racks can be restored.

The line under the counters says how many equipment rooms, IDFs, COWs, AV network nodes and Wi-Fi points are on the map and on which pages; those are managed in the Drawing viewer.

Step 5

Report (PDF)

ControlWhat it does
Marked-up PDFThe book with everything drawn on it — racks with their reach circles, equipment rooms, IDFs, COWs, AV network nodes, Wi-Fi points, named areas — and the legend on the first page. It runs in the background with a progress bar; on a large book it takes a minute or two.
with legendOff, the legend box is left out and the circles, the scale and the disclaimer go to a thin line at the foot of each page.
Composite map PDFOne page per composite map you built (below).
Composite map

Composite map

The card under the exports builds, on request, one map per level of one book: the detail sheets laid side by side (never two series, never the key plans), with every rack, its reach circles and the Wi-Fi points already drawn on the sheets. It opens in a window of its own. On it you can move, delete and reclassify racks; a move follows the rack on every sheet that shows the new spot, with undo. Sheets that cannot be placed automatically appear beside the map and can be moved by hand.

Account & security

Account & security

What it does not do

  • It reads no demand list and counts nothing: no ports, no demand, no capacity, no served spaces.
  • It traces no cable routes and links no levels; every distance is a straight line on one sheet.
  • It shares no project, account or package with RackReach.
  • It sends nothing anywhere: it runs on this computer only.
