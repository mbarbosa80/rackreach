Origin

The RackReach Map is the map half of RackReach, taken out as an application of its own on 5 November 2026 from that application's version 5.3.0. The full analyzer crosses the venue design books with the demand list; this one is for studying the books alone — placing racks, measuring, looking at their reach — without any link to the demand list, so the two kinds of work are never mixed.

The two applications are kept apart: their own installation, address, tasks, projects and accounts. A change in the full analyzer that affects the map (reading the books, the viewer, the marked-up PDF, the composite map) is brought here when it is needed; nothing flows the other way.
